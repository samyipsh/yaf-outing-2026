How the morning works

  • Each round: 15 minutes to play, then 5 to walk to your next stop. Station ICs keep time, so move on when they say.
  • Each station ranks the groups: first gets 300 points and last gets 60, and tied groups share. Every photo that counts adds its challenge’s points.
  • The highest total wins and picks their bubble tea first. A tie goes to the group with more photo points.

Puzzle card

  • Your group IC gets the card at the briefing, with your first hint and Type of Christ. Every station gives you one more pair, whatever you score.
  • Stick each pair in its slot, and write a sentence on why they match and how that person or thing points to Christ.
  • It isn’t scored. We go through the answers together at the results.

Photo quest

  • Pick your group in the app. Anyone in the group can send photos.
  • The judge checks photos during the morning. One that doesn’t count comes back with a reason, and you can retake it.
  • Awards for most animals (name each one), most flower colours, most photos and most liked photo.

Photo rules

  • Photos only. Don’t pick plants, catch insects or feed animals.
  • Ask before you photograph someone’s dog.
  • Don’t climb trees or stand on railings or benches. Stay out of the water and back from the lake edge.
  • Skip Jacob Ballas Children’s Garden: it doesn’t let in adults without children.
  • Photos go into a gallery everyone at the outing can see, and a shared album afterwards. Tell your photo IC if you’d rather not be in photos.

Bring

A charged phone and a power bank, a water bottle, an umbrella, insect repellent, and a cap and sunglasses.

Safety

  • Emergency: call 995, then tell your group IC.
  • Separated? Stay where you are and message your group IC. No reply in 10 minutes: go to the Trellis Garden gazebo.
  • Running late? Go to the Trellis Garden gazebo and find Samuel or Eshem.
  • First aid and drinks are at the Trellis Garden gazebo, and Eshem carries a second first-aid kit.
  • Drink water between rounds. It gets hot by late morning.

If it rains

  • Light rain: we carry on. Most stations are under shelters.
  • Thunder or lightning: stop playing and go into the nearest building, not an open gazebo.
  • Heavy rain or a storm that won’t pass: we’ll send the wet-weather plan on WhatsApp.
